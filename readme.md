# Etherreality Genesis
**By CaptAnby**  |  Version 1.0.0

A cinematic, photorealistic shader pack for Iris and OptiFine.

## Features
- Volumetric clouds, 3D volumetric fog and atmospheric haze
- Realistic water with waves, reflections, caustics and soft horizon blending
- Physically based sky, sun and moon, real constellations and a night-sky nebula
- Real-time shadows, PBR materials, block reflections and optional ray-traced shadows
- Weather effects: puddles, lens rain drops and storm effects
- The End: pulsar, volumetric nebula fog, nebula clouds and pillar light beams
- Six presets: Potato, Low, Medium, High, Ultra and Ether (each preset sets its own values)
- Languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Korean, Filipino

## Installation
1. Install Iris (recommended) or OptiFine.
2. Place the .zip file in the `shaderpacks` folder, without extracting it.
3. Select Etherreality Genesis in Options > Video Settings > Shader Packs.

## Settings
Settings are grouped into Lighting, Shadows, Sky, Sun & Moon, Clouds, Atmosphere & Fog, Water,
Weather, Reflections, Materials, Post-Processing, Nether & Environment and The End.
Detailed options are under Advanced Settings. Hover over an option to see its description.
